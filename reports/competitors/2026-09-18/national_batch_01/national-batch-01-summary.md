## Interpretation limits

The searches are fixed and reproducible but discovery-bounded; they are not a complete business registry. Only operator-validated physical branches are counted. A physical branch may fall inside more than one MRT catchment, so node counts cannot be summed into a unique branch total. Eight nodes are not enough to compare candidate and audit distributions, test H1, amend the frozen shortlist or make a final recommendation.
