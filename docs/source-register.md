## Stage-specific blockers and limitations

1. **No licensed, reproducible source of current unit-level commercial availability and asking rent has been established.** CommercialGuru and 99.co expose useful fields to a human viewer, but their terms prohibit the systematic collection/reuse needed here. The HDB vacancy page opened, but its dynamic inventory and reuse terms could not be inspected. This blocks current-unit comparison and lease-ready recommendations, not conditional commercial-node shortlisting. S016 is restricted manual context only; its observations are not a publishable analytical input and cannot support node-specific rent ranges.
2. **No premises can yet be `verified_eligible`.** URA and SCDF publish usable screening rules, but final premises status requires current unit-specific evidence: approved use/change-of-use position, owner/landlord consent and applicable SCDF/QP/FSC evidence. Current availability and quoted/all-in cost are separate lease-readiness evidence. School/operator registration and applicability under the Education Act/MOE process, and ordinary commercial/lease due diligence, are also separate layers; URA/SCDF evidence alone does not establish universal legal readiness. This does not prevent a conditional node recommendation.
3. **Public-transport routing is usable only with an explicit per-route fallback.** The fixed S007 preflight executed all 13 searches and 60 route calls. Walking succeeded for 30/30 pairs; PT succeeded for 19/30 and returned HTTP 404/missing route for 11/30. No request was rate-limited. S007 remains `verified_limited`; every missing PT result must stay missing and use the documented walking/proximity fallback rather than imputation.
4. **The preferred small-area population source is processed with its rounding limitation preserved.** Full S001 ingestion found 107,088 data rows plus five note rows, zero duplicate `(Planning Area, Subzone, Age, Sex)` keys, the expected age/sex categories and no invalid target-age counts. Total-sex ages 7–16 produced 3,320 age rows and 332 subzone proxies. All 332 name pairs matched S003 exactly after case/whitespace normalization. Published counts remain rounded to 10: the subzone target-age sum is 412,630 versus the published national aggregate 412,010, a 620 difference that is reported rather than corrected. Resource-specific raw redistribution permission remains unestablished.
5. **There is no authoritative national registry of current P1–S4 mathematics tuition branches and offerings.** Nationwide discovery must combine multiple channels with operator pages and disclose coverage limits. Complete manual validation is required for branches affecting shortlisted catchments; exhaustive national manual validation is an optional extension. Until the inventory and audit are executed, competition coverage is partial.

## Refresh and conflict policy

- Dynamic competitor, offering, fee and premises evidence must be rechecked within **30 days** of the analysis cutoff; every shortlisted competitor branch and premises is rechecked on the decision date.
- Official transport/site datasets must be the latest published version available at acquisition. Annual demographics use the latest release; the 2020 census fallback always carries a stale-vintage flag.
- Land-use, fire-safety and school/operator-registration guidance is rechecked within **90 days** of a premises decision, while unit-specific permissions/owner consent and listing availability are rechecked within **30 days**.
- Preserve conflicts. Operator evidence controls branch offering; authoritative unit documents control premises eligibility; directory/listing assertions never override either. An unresolved material conflict remains `uncertain`/`unresolved` and blocks the affected comparison.
